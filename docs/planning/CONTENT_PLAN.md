## Boss encounter requirements

Greenkeeper has mortar windups, sweeping attacks, exposed weak points and three recoverable phases. Synchronicity rotates dish attack lanes and has safe cover windows. Amalgam teleports between pods with warning cues and interruptible attacks. Mandela Architect switches validated arena geometry and exposes its core through pattern recognition. The Earth portal warden precedes eleven distinct guardians. After all receipts, Core Prime and Archon of Silence have separate full health stages; the planned bounded finale terminal interaction still requires its own acceptance.

Each boss has an intro card, phase data, telegraphs, health/death behavior, retry checkpoint, reward event and audio/visual feedback. Tune numbers in balance.json and test reset from every phase.
