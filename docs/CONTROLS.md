# Current prototype controls

**World travel:** After each guardian conversation, a saved galaxy map and vortex play automatically for about 4 seconds. After the initial brief guard, Enter, Space, left click or controller A skips the sequence. F2/Start pauses; a save failure displays a retry prompt. Continue replays interrupted travel. CRT off does not disable the animation.

Launch from the repository with `/opt/anaconda3/envs/6equj5_game/bin/python -m video_game.main`, or install the game extra and use `6equj5-game`. Python 3.12 is the tested game runtime. `--dev` enables optional inspection shortcuts; none of the controls below requires it.

**Earth and signal journey:** Move with Left/Right, jump with Space and aim/fire with the mouse. In Assisted mode holding Right crosses forward field exits; Up or the nearby prompt also uses an exit. After the Earth portal warden, each alien field leads to a guardian; after victory, Enter/Space or click advances the two information pages. The final page waits for your acknowledgement, saves the receipt, then teleports to the next world. After all eleven receipts, the Earth AI has two full health stages. Continue starts in the bunker; leaving resumes the saved route or pending message. If a save fails, follow the on-screen retry prompt before continuing. FASR-001 and FASR-003 realm/guardian art are bounded visual benchmarks; the other nine realm backgrounds and guardian visuals remain temporary fallbacks. The eleven minor-foe bodies are separate.

**Easier combat:** Restart the game to load the update. Your saved combat profile is preserved; switch to Assisted under **F2 → COMBAT** if it is currently Classic. Fresh settings default to Assisted. The chosen profile persists. In field and boss scenes, point with the mouse and hold the left button to repeat the starter maser; hold and release the right button for a charged shot. The X/J/Left Ctrl keyboard fire/charge action remains available. Assisted starter fire remains five closely grouped rays; Classic uses one. Other unlocked weapons keep their own firing and charging behavior.

**Mapped controller:** Left stick or D-pad moves; right stick aims; RT repeats starter-maser shots, LT charges/releases. A jumps, B dashes, X interacts, Y grapples, LB cycles weapons, RB activates the selected ability, and Start pauses. Back opens the bunker dossier. Controller A starts/continues, goes outside from the bunker, and advances the guided receiver and lab; B goes back in supported menus. D-pad/A/B navigate the supported title, pause and bunker dossier controls. The pause settings and guided buttons can also be clicked with the mouse. Archive switching and advanced panels still need keyboard or mouse. Controller events have been tested with simulated devices; physical hardware compatibility and a controller-only playthrough remain unverified.

In Assisted mode, hostile attacks are reduced, defeated non-boss enemies restore up to 5 health once while Mateo is alive, returning to the safe bunker restores health, and dish pressure is gentler; environmental hazards remain dangerous. Classic preserves its original hostile damage and defense rates. The on-screen reticle and projectiles show angled aim; the current Mateo sprite faces left or right and does not yet have a separate angled weapon pose. Opening a modal, losing focus or disconnecting a controller cancels interrupted charging rather than releasing a shot.

After receiving and validating FASR-001, travel through the approach, perimeter road, Ohio campus and outer salvage to collect the coupler in the salvage yard. In Assisted mode, holding right automatically crosses available forward field exits, carrying movement and normal repeat fire; story gates still apply and boss entry remains explicit. Continue right to the catwalk, then press Up, E or controller X, or click the nearby prompt, to install it and start the defense. The bunker repair bench remains an alternate route. After the defense, continue right through the reflector basin road and use the boss entrance to confront Greenkeeper. Aim for the exposed green reactor when it is vulnerable. The victory pages reflect earned records and return to the bunker. After Act I, the connected route continues through Neo-Denver, Area 51, VLA/Dodleston and Göbekli before the alien worlds; saved progress is preserved. With an uninstalled coupler, the approach gives a repair reminder; after repair, departure routes to the catwalk, and after defense it routes to the boss.

If a checkpoint save fails, its warning remains visible. Press F5 in the field to retry; on the victory story, F5, Enter or the next action retries before leaving. Movement turns Mateo toward travel direction when not firing; dedicated authored backpedal animations are not present.

The reality annex and relay/flashback links offer additional prototype mechanics alongside the connected campaign. Weapon/ability unlocks derive from saved validation and event records. New/Continue reconstructs temporary actors, projectiles, energy and cooldowns; room edits, collected pickups, relay flags, validated scenarios and rewards persist. Incomplete lab trials reset on changing scenario or leaving the lab. Returning to the bunker delivers one pending science consequence notice per visit. Continue starts safely in the bunker.

For disposable testing set `SIX_GAME_SAVE_DIR` to a temporary directory. Real audio, graphical performance and full fresh-save campaign playthrough are separate acceptance checks; headless tests do not establish them.

In the reality annex, N places the next pair only where the active timeline has safe space and support. If no pair can be placed, a warning appears and the current encounter remains. A successful cycle clears existing encounter projectiles.

**Temporary weapons:** Walk through a gold-framed supply cell to equip **Arc Lance** (90 damage, pierces 2 targets, 18 seconds) or **Nova Scatter** (three-way spread, 70 damage per target, 16 seconds). Hold mouse fire, X/J/Left Ctrl or a controller trigger to repeat shots. The upper-left countdown freezes while paused; expiry restores the starter maser automatically. A new cell replaces the current boost without stacking. Q/LB cannot bypass its timer. Death/retry, room changes, New and Continue clear temporary equipment; permanent unlocks are unchanged. Supplies and pickups reset per room visit. Dish-catwalk supplies start beyond the defense area.

**Action colors:** Cyan/white electricity and hit sparks, warm orange destruction and hurt responses, gold supply/checkpoint indicators. Damage numbers, expanding rings and local matching light respond to actual hits; background motes stay subdued.
