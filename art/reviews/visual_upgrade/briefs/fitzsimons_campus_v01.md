# Fictional Ohio campus: Fitzsimons-inspired architecture v01

**Status: v02 installed and accepted as a bounded far-background component; full-room art gate remains open.** One candidate architecture layer for the existing Act I campus service edge, not a new room or a real-world location transfer. [Visual research](../references/fitzsimons_campus_v01/research.md); [existing campus brief](ohio_campus_extension_v01.md).

## Visual goal

Behind the side-scrolling action, put one monumental but distant fictional tan-brick institutional building: broad balanced wings stepping up toward a central tower, three tall dark vertical gridded window strips, restrained pale horizontal stone bands and a recessed pale stone entrance. Borrow the reference's Art Deco/Modernistic rhythm rather than trace its exact footprint. Layer small Ohio tree silhouettes, a slender distant flagpole and modest campus planting/service details in front of it to give the setting depth. Vary the wings and surrounding detail enough that travel reveals a coherent place, not a repeated facade. No lettering, logos, new characters or recognizable real-campus map.

Night: a deep cool blue-violet sky within the new far plate; a shadowed warm-tan building whose silhouette and pale trim are legible against it; a few warm windows and restrained, localized amber entrance/lamp halos. Deliberately cluster light, leave many windows dark, and reserve the strongest warning colors and contrast for gameplay. Avoid full-screen bloom, busy window noise, photographic sky, dramatic foreground spotlights or a uniformly bright building. Relative depth and scale should read as a large institution set behind the small existing player, not a building wall at actor depth. Keep windows, entrance and trees visually subordinate to actors and active cues.

Create exactly one original candidate master. Preserve actual output dimensions, prompt, source and SHA-256; a master is not an approved runtime export. Do not stretch nonuniformly, tile/repeat the facade, enlarge insufficient detail silently, or crop away its identity just to meet the export contract. If a usable registered full-width layer cannot be made from this candidate, retain it as a concept and report the limitation instead of claiming a finished layer. This direction alone did not authorize promotion; the subsequent reviewed v02 integration is recorded below.

Inspect opacity and boundaries; then registered actual-scene crops at both camera extremes and intermediate positions with terrain, platforms, players, enemies, hazards, exits, effects and HUD visible, CRT off and on. Check the warm halos against player/projectiles, platform alignment and hazard cues, wing continuity, roofline intersections, source scaling, boundaries, edge seams, and movement/parallax separately. Static fixture crops are not human-play, native-window, performance or full-room art-gate acceptance. Preserve the installed campus family, all source masters and recovery material.

## Installed outcome — 2026-11-12

Root rejected v01's broadly warm, bright facade but accepted v02's cooler nighttime depth as a bounded far component after registered and installed-root review. Its dark tower bands and missing central far lamps are limitations; the unchanged foreground supplies warm practical lighting. The fictional Ohio location, world plane, gameplay and geometry remain unchanged. [Final report](../previews/fitzsimons_campus_v01/final_report.md) records source/export transforms, hashes, tests, stills, sampled movement and recovery. Fixture inspection does not establish native-window or human-play review or complete-room art acceptance.
