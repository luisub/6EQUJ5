Create warm, layered **Ohio Big Ear service-yard architecture**, with masonry and patched concrete wall masses, a recognizable recessed service bay, electrical cabinets, pipes and a modest cable reel connected by a consistent physical structure. Use broad near-black shadow shapes and cool ambient contrasted with a few warm practical lamps; strong amber light shapes across specific masonry/metal surfaces, not a uniform gradient, bloom or generic fog. Establish variation over the whole 960-unit room so right-side travel does not fall into blank repetition. Do not copy the flattened approved study verbatim; keep the same visual direction while solving its gameplay conflicts.
