# Ohio campus service edge — background master v01

**Status: bounded campus background installed; full-room art gate open.** The approved blockout adds a fictional 2029 Core-occupied service edge between the approach and salvage yard. The Ohio research packet identifies Big Ear as a Kraus-type reflector, demolished in 1998; the operating 2029 site and Core presence are game fiction. Perkins-inspired low masonry, an optical dome, pale trim, working service features and Ohio tree silhouettes inform the design without reproducing photographs, logos or an actual campus layout. Research photographs are reference only and are not cleared production assets.

No scenery should suggest an extra traversable platform, cover, hazard, interaction or exit.

At the original integration checkpoint, the approved far plate was a separate opaque component, reused byte-for-byte: `assets/environments/ohio_approach_v1/far_1152x544.png`. The commissioned layer is **one continuous world-fixed, finite, nonrepeating transparent architecture image**, target native export 2560×576 straight-alpha RGBA, source origin world (0,0), parallax (1,1), camera crop `(2*round(x), 2*round(y), 960, 540)`. Do not paint sky, terrain, actors or actual platforms. Alpha follows natural architecture silhouettes, not artificial holes around gameplay. Full coverage and registration must be verified; naming, packaging and runtime integration await review. A source master is not an export.

## Visual direction

Create three distinct adjoining beats: a western service gate and small observatory wing; a central research court, varied low masonry and a modest white optical dome; an eastern loading edge with a fenced maintenance vehicle and waste station. These are fictional service settings, not literal geography. Distant parked car/van and tiny maintenance workers may be baked into the backdrop as static, noninteractive details. Workers do not establish animation. Quiet machinery, conduit and cabinets convey use; avoid making background silhouettes read as enemies or cover. Use cool twilight shadows, restrained amber practical pools and selective cyan Core accents; retain real gameplay warnings as the strongest cues. Describe materials with deliberate clusters and coherent lighting, not noise, blur or blanket glow. Avoid copied signage or readable generated lettering.

## Generation and review

Commission exactly one original panoramic master, ideally 3840×864 (40:9) and at least 2560 px wide, transparent outside natural silhouettes. The image tool may not obey dimensions. Record actual dimensions, alpha, exact prompt, references and SHA-256. Do not nonuniformly stretch, repeat a facade, enlarge insufficient source detail, or silently crop out meaningful architecture to meet the target. If a single output lacks usable aspect or native-width coverage, preserve it as a concept and stop; propose two registered overlapping original panels, commissioned one at a time after review.

Inspect alpha on light and dark, purposeful native-sized edges and clusters, and registered crops at camera start, intermediate positions and both ends with actual terrain, players, enemies, hazards, exits, effects and HUD. Motion, CRT on/off, parallax, wrapping, gameplay fairness and human review are separate checks; a static master does not establish them. Preserve all existing masters, runtime families and saves. No retirement or asset transaction is authorized by this brief alone.
