# Firing-light benchmark draft review — v01

2026-11-12. Read-only inspection of `briefs/firing_lighting_benchmark_v03.md` and the first opt-in draft of `engine/firing_lighting.py`, `engine/scenes/field.py`, `engine/effects.py` and `engine/crt_renderer.py`. The draft is work in progress; findings are source observations and risks, not results of runtime captures or tests. Sent to technical owner while implementation continues.

## Concrete issues to resolve

1. **Weapon and aim eligibility.** Draft `active_flashes` selects matching room token and `not is_charged`. Field assigns that token to every new projectile. Uncharged scattergun, EMP and low-stage coilgun therefore qualify for the new burst and have their previous flash suppressed; non-horizontal starter shots also qualify despite the horizontal-only authored burst. This conflicts with v03's normal horizontal starter-maser tap scope. Base any tag on the actual spawned projectile's weapon, charged state and direction at discharge, not merely the current selected weapon or player facing. Preserve the legacy path for every ineligible event, including a projectile still active when the weapon changes.
2. **Lamp placement.** The measured native centers are approximately (257,389) and (1082,441), but the draft top edges of recovery ellipses are y=410 and y=450 respectively. Therefore the measured fixtures themselves lie above their corresponding masks. The first measured pool begins around y=350, the second around y=405. Compare against the actual registered art and camera positions; these measurements are approximate and not gameplay metadata.
3. **Composite-wide treatment.** The proposed lamp recovery and additive shot field act on a fully composited world image, not a material mask. They can restore/brighten parallax sky, empty air and collision tiles in the same projected region and present as oval light patches. The ambient multiply also affects actual hazard tile colors. Source inspection establishes those pixels are included in the pass; whether the result is visually unacceptable requires actual scene captures. Protect receptive material and preserve hazard readability rather than assuming that being behind enemies protects all gameplay cues.

## Positive boundaries and remaining checks

- The field places the world pass before enemy telegraphs and later HUD and preserves the existing native player depth. Actor bounce is called only within `not flash_hide`; it RGB-adds to a copy of the sprite, which is intended to retain alpha and avoid modifying the animation cache. Verify exact alpha bytes, original cached sprite bytes and invulnerability blink in actual tests.
- Ambient/no-shot behavior is intentionally darker only when explicitly enabled and in the approach. Disabled, absent asset, charged shot, other room and room transition paths need comparison against legacy output. Room token increments on `load_room`, so prior flashes cannot drive the **new** current-token field; `EffectManager.render` may still draw an old nonmatching flash through its legacy branch, so test that behavior separately rather than claim every residual is eliminated.
- Masks and burst variants are precomputed per lighting instance: nine world levels, nine actor levels, two practical masks and 16 burst variants, plus one native viewport ambient surface. Bound aggregate effects, per-shot lifetime and frame cost in the actual benchmark; cache existence alone does not establish unchanged frame budget or readability. World lights use rounded screen coordinates whereas native-world crop is `2*round(camera)`: fractional positions can shift registration relative to world art; test near half-pixel boundaries and viewport edges.
- World glow and burst are anchored to the recorded new projectile spawn, preserving gameplay origin. Rendering after weather and before HUD preserves interface layering but the flash image itself can still overlap an enemy telegraph or beam, requiring an actual overlap review. Actor clipping/caching and shadow shape must be inspected at native scale and with CRT enabled/disabled.

Technical owner was notified of items 1–3; no code, assets or tests were changed by this review. The initial audit and source/fixture evidence are `report.md` in the same directory. Await the revised implementation and actual captures before assessing visual quality or completion.
