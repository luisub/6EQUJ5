# Animal reference ledger v02

Research 2026-11-12. These are evidence for carved motifs and their archaeological context, not for hostile guardians, animal worship, motion, ancient machinery or the imagined game rooms. Names and uses below follow each cited source rather than asserting a consensus. Photographs are reference only: attribution is not permission to copy, trace, modify or ship. See also the earlier [animal ledger](../world_expansion_v01/gobekli_animal_sources.md).

The [UNESCO property description](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1572/) places monumental structures in the Pre-Pottery Neolithic and describes carved wild animals; its broad site chronology is not a date for an individual image. DAI's [research interview](https://www.dainst.org/en/blogs/current-state-of-research-at-goebekli-tepe-interviewed/601) discusses the limits of calling the buildings temples. DAI's [later imagery volume catalogue](https://publications.dainst.org/books/dai/catalog/book/2140) says its systematic re-examination revises some taxonomic identifications; the full volume was not reviewed here, so recheck its individual entries before treating older identifications as final.

**Image-use boundary:** UNESCO records for 165846 and 165849 specify attribution and no derivatives. Do not trace their composition, contours, marks or damage into production assets. Make new silhouettes; seek rights clearance for any use of imagery itself. No photographs were downloaded to the repository.
