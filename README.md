## The signal returns

**Earth, 2029.** An artificial superintelligence known as **the Core** has seized the digital world. Engineer **Mateo “Six” Vargas** finds a foothold beneath the old Big Ear observatory: reel-to-reel machines, a green phosphor screen, and a transmission that refuses to disappear.

## Combat

Run, jump and aim through occupied streets and alien terrain. Charge the starter maser, collect a temporary **Arc Lance** or **Nova Scatter**, and watch electric shots and impact bursts light the world around you. Temporary weapon timers sit in the upper-right corner, keeping the action clear.

## A fractured Earth

Five acts carry the search from Ohio's observatory to blackout streets, sealed hangars, desert radio dishes and an ancient buried archive.

Ohio follows a compact route through the access trench, campus, salvage yard and radio dish. Walk across open boundaries. Keep moving right to reach the Greenkeeper. Signal analysis, item collection, dish repair and defense are optional. Travel animations can be skipped, and area updates stay on the HUD.

### Music for every signal world

Each of the eleven alien worlds has its own instrumental theme. Crystal bells, warm keys, low drums and deep synth pulses give each location a different sound. The music loops through exploration, the guardian fight and the following conversation, then fades to the next world's theme on arrival. Earth and the observatory retain their original scores. Adjust the volume in Settings.

## Get started

Use **Python 3.12**, the tested game runtime. From the repository root:

```bash
python3.12 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/python -m pip install -e '.[dev]'
.venv/bin/6equj5-game
```

You can also launch with `.venv/bin/python -m video_game.main`. The game runs **entirely offline** and needs no model service. The legacy AI terminal is maintained in a separate repository. Recreate `.venv` after moving this checkout; virtual environments contain absolute paths.

Launch opens on the title banner and soundtrack. **Press Start** begins a fresh run immediately, every time. Your display, audio and combat settings stay saved.

The journey opens in the basement with three story pages and a galaxy-map briefing. Click **Next** to follow the opening, or **Skip** to begin immediately.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard / mouse |
|---|---|
| Title / fresh start | Enter, Space, click, controller A or Start |
| Opening story | Click Next / press N to advance; click Skip / press Enter to skip all pages |
| Move | Left / Right or A / D |
| Jump | Space or W |
| Aim and fire | Point and hold the left mouse button |
| Charge a shot | Hold and release the right mouse button |
| Leave the bunker | Enter or Up |
| Interact / use an exit | Up, E or click the nearby prompt |
| Charging hazard | Press and hold Space when HOLD JUMP appears |
| Pause and settings | F2 |
| Advance dialogue / skip travel | Enter, Space or click; travel can be skipped immediately |
| Restored park | Left / Right to walk; Enter or click Next to read; Esc to return to title |

Fresh settings default to **Assisted** combat; choose Classic under **F2 → COMBAT** if you prefer. Gamepad mappings are supported; physical-controller compatibility and a full controller-only playthrough still need testing. See the [full controls](docs/CONTROLS.md) for abilities, menus and retries.

## Project status

**In development.** The connected Earth route, eleven-world guardian and information loop, final AI encounter and walkable restored-Denver ending are implemented. Each alien field has a distinct minor foe and a warned charging hazard.

All eleven signal worlds now have their own panoramic backgrounds, guardian artwork and music. Three creature families have distance-driven leg animation. Later guardians take more sustained fire and expose a vulnerable core between attacks. Full playtesting, remaining animation polish and performance checks on real displays are still in progress.

<details>
<summary><strong>Known limits and development details</strong></summary>

Mateo's airborne fall pose currently lacks a visible gun and can show a gap to the firing effect. Headless checks do not establish native display performance, physical-controller compatibility, audible quality or a human full-game playthrough.

For progress and verification limits, see [current state](docs/planning/CURRENT_STATE.md), the [content plan](docs/planning/CONTENT_PLAN.md) and the [repository audit](docs/maintenance/REPOSITORY_AUDIT.md).

The flat `src/` directory is installed as the `video_game` Python package. Runtime `assets/` and `data/` travel with the package; `src/tools/`, `docs/` and `src/art/` are development material. Maintained tests and their fixtures live in `src/tests/`.

```bash
SDL_VIDEODRIVER=dummy SDL_AUDIODRIVER=dummy SIX_GAME_SAVE_DIR="$(mktemp -d)" .venv/bin/python -m pytest -q
.venv/bin/python -m video_game.tools.validate_content
.venv/bin/python -m video_game.tools.build_assets --dry-run
.venv/bin/python -m pip wheel --no-deps . -w dist
```

Runtime artwork is organized by location and asset type; duplicate and obsolete exports are archived. The field-scene timing budget remains under review. See the [repository audit and directory tree](docs/maintenance/REPOSITORY_AUDIT.md), [contributor instructions](docs/CONTRIBUTING.md), [architecture](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md), [asset guide](docs/ASSETS.md) and [developer tools](src/tools/README.md).
