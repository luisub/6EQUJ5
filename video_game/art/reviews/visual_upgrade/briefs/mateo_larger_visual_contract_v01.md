# Mateo larger appearance — render-only contract v01

**Proposed implementation and review contract, not installed art or a physics change.** User authorized a modest increase in apparent size. The current 60×80 idle preview came from the preserved generated master; it is one static direction candidate, not an approved animation set. No additional frames or runtime files are created by this document.

## Existing caller and scale facts

`FieldOpsScene` and `BossScene` load `assets/sprites/mateo/manifest.json` olive-field clips through `engine/animation.py`. A 96×128 manifest cell with pivot `(48,124)` uses `AnimationPlayer.scale_factor=0.25`: 24×32 logical cell / **48×64 native render cell**. Scene render computes world foot `(player.x + width/2, player.y + 32)` and inserts the native surface at Mateo's existing z-order; field projectiles are later, boss projectiles earlier. `composite_native` resamples to twice the supplied logical size, so passing the old 24×32 logical size alongside a 60×80 image would shrink it back to 48×64. Bunker is separate: `BunkerHubScene` loads strips / a 96×128 presentation sprite and has its own position, shadow and layer order; a field/boss proposal does not automatically alter it.

`scale_factor` also transforms clip foot pivots, `get_attachment_world_pos` and animation hurtbox. `engine/weapons.py:get_muzzle_world_pos` and `Player.get_muzzle_world_pos` use the animator attachment; projectile and muzzle effects follow those world coordinates. **Do not increase `scale_factor` as a shortcut:** that moves gameplay attachment/hurtbox metadata and is not render-only. Player collision uses `Player.rect` and the physical 16×32 standing, crouch and slide/dash footprints, independently of the visual sprite. Preserve `.25` gameplay scale, world positions, collision/hurtboxes, projectile origins, speed, physics, input and saves.

## Reviewed still and envelope

`previews/mateo_large_cell_v02/` samples the original 1086×1448 generated master directly, not the small export. Static cell **60×80**, right-facing pivot **(30,78)**, destination **(82,366)** at camera `(0,18)`, screen foot **(112,444)**. Its alpha>127 bbox `(16,8,44,79)` gives **28×71** substantive pixels versus earlier selected preview 22×57, a **24.6% height increase**. The original current-runtime 48×64 source-derived visible bbox was 25×59. The standing player collision at that camera is native screen `(96,380,32,64)`; preview visible bbox `(98,374,28,71)` reaches six pixels above and one row below that rectangle under half-open bounds. This is artwork overhang, not a larger hitbox. The bbox is specific to this one pose, alpha threshold and fixture, not a safe envelope for crouch, slide, jump or weapons.

For a general renderer experiment, derive each native visual frame and its visual pivot independently of gameplay metadata; consistent 1.25× around the world foot is the starting comparison, not automatic approval of all silhouettes. Right-facing 60×80 pivot is `(30,78)` for the current default source pivot; under a horizontally flipped cell the corresponding x is `60−1−30=29`. Use clip-specific pivots for any state that differs; round only at the native placement boundary and test the existing camera rounding behavior. Supply the new visual dimensions to the compositor rather than changing gameplay animator scale. Preserve actual field/boss depth, flash/blink, pause, camera clipping and fallback behavior. Test on the real native surfaces with actual room layers, not just a scaled inspection snapshot.

## Muzzle, hand and weapon continuity

For the default idle manifest, foot `(48,124)`, muzzle `(108,44)` and hand `(76,52)` produce world offsets **(+15,−20)** and **(+7,−18)** at `.25`; the muzzle is intentionally outside the 96-wide source cell and must not be clamped. At the example native foot `(112,444)`, current right-facing gameplay muzzle is `(142,404)` and hand `(126,408)`; mirrored left-facing is `(82,404)` and `(98,408)`. These values illustrate unchanged gameplay anchors for that clip only; recoil, jump and other clips use different attachments. The static concept is unarmed and does not verify any held weapon, aim direction or muzzle.

Enlarging a drawn torso/arm around the foot can move the visible hand and barrel away from these unchanged world anchors. Review each state and facing against actual projectile origin, muzzle flash and weapon aim; use authored render-only registration/hand and barrel placement or a separately reviewed weapon presentation to make them align without moving gameplay anchors. Do not move physics or silently retarget projectiles merely to match a larger picture. If clear alignment is impossible under the requested envelope, surface that concrete design decision; do not claim the preview is ready.

## Scope and required checks

The olive manifest has 15 clips: idle 4, walk 2, run 4, jump, fall, land, crouch, slide, cover low/background, shoot, recoil, tune, hurt and death. `Player.animation_state` currently chooses idle, walk, run, jump/fall, crouch, slide, cover low/background, recoil and death based on actual game state; the mere existence of land/shoot/tune/hurt clips does not prove those states have a normal runtime caller. Audit real callers and preserve manifest semantics; do not invent or claim playback. Ordinary steady movement targets walk speed and selects the existing two-frame walk; longer route distance and better walk motion are separate problems.

Before a production update, inspect consistent frame-to-frame foot contact, scale, anatomy, clearance and alpha on light/dark backgrounds, both facings, actual playback and screen edges, roof/low clearance, platforms and one-way landings, crouch/slide/cover, jump/fall, recoil and aiming, death/respawn, weapon muzzle and effects, all earned projectile types, HUD/telegraphs, enemy overlap, blink, pause and scene transitions. Account for the overhang in fairness review: art can visually enter a wall, hazard or attack while the fixed collider does not. Test field and boss separately, including their differing layer order; test bunker only if that distinct scene is changed. Meaningful normal-input tests and explicit screenshots/motion are required alongside per-frame inspection. Native pixel clusters/alpha cleanup remains needed for the candidate; no auto-upscaled or procedural frames are finished art.

Stage any reviewed new art in a new family with recorded master provenance, exact cell/pivot/attachment declarations, hashes and verified originals. Integrate only through the coordinated safe asset transaction; leave old frames as recovery/fallback. Document the specific actual scope and visual/collision difference. No one still, alpha count or test suite alone closes motion, fairness, art gate, G2/G3 or native performance acceptance.
