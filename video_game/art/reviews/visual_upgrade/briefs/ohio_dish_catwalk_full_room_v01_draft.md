## Visual composition

- **Far:** an immense receiver/reflector structure legible as grouped dark masses, with sky and sparse details. Avoid dense repeating wire over HUD and trajectories.
- **Game plane:** true catwalks retain exact lips, span and collision. Structural supports sit behind these spans and align without suggesting extra horizontal footing or climbable ladders. Keep the y=240 floor and cover silhouettes faithful; a dramatic shadow cannot hide a safe landing.
- **Feedhorn:** large enough to orient a player towards the actual x=320 interaction, with a grounded/supporting structure behind existing geometry; avoid a fake panel, perpetual jam beacon or a lamp that resembles targeting. Make mechanical materials read differently from the surrounding structure while keeping actual HUD, prompt, enemies and projectiles highest priority.
- **Light and depth:** cool distant reflector and near-black service structure, occasional directional amber work lamps illuminating steel joints, cable conduits or concrete, with deliberate shadow shapes. Preserve open visual paths for aerial enemies and player projectiles; bright warning/telegraph colors stay reserved for real cues. The result is an exposed scientific installation, not another garage, salvage heap, vehicle scene or fantasy environment.
