## Goal and fixed contract

Build a coherent, dramatic retrofuturistic Ohio radio-observatory service yard: deep near-black shadow masses, selective warm practical lamps and lit brick/concrete/steel against cool twilight, broad architectural forms and deliberate material detail. Continue the approved composition's scientific identity, boxy period maintenance vehicle, varied cabinets, pipes, bins and cable reel while fixing its specific problems: wagon must not be intersected by a real catwalk or warning, catwalks need believable non-climbable support, and the sump must never reveal a fake floor. The user approved direction, not unreviewed runtime assets. No fantasy/isometric scene or uniform blue haze.

Room `data/levels/act_01/approach.json` is 960×288 world units with 16-unit tiles. World view remains 480×270; final density is 2 native pixels per world unit. Camera x spans **0–480**, y spans **0–18**. Retain all collisions, spawns, objects, exits and HUD at their real positions. The selected old still camera `(0,18)` shows only the left half: all later assets and lighting must also work at the middle and right limits. Existing loaded backgrounds currently use 0.10/0.05, 0.35/0.15, 0.65/0.25 x/y ratios; room metadata differs. Proposed new camera sampling below is a new contract, not a claim that old motion or phase is identical.

At a minimum preserve originals and hashes; compare x=0, x=240, x=480 and both reachable y limits, at 960 native and 1920 nearest 2×, with actual room tiles/objects/actors/HUD, no fabricated features. Once integrated, run affected loader/render tests and project checkpoint, content validation and safe asset dry-run; document commands, sources, candidate status and unresolved limits. No candidate becomes a locked production export merely because a plate looks good.
