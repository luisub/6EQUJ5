It is a vehicle parked in the background **behind** actors and foreground room tiles, not a drivable car, pickup, obstacle, cover, climbable surface or interaction. No lettering, logos, lamps, warning-colored beacons, artificial cast shadow across gameplay or added floor. Give it a subdued value range distinct from Mateo and projectiles; preserve warm/cool material relationship with the reviewed building, but do not add effects or extra props. One coherent subject only.

Proposed cell **112×64 native pixels** = **56×32 world units**, with transparent straight-alpha exterior and no background. Position cell world rectangle `(804,208,56,32)` / full-room native rectangle `(1608,416,112,64)`; bottom contact line is world y=240 / native y=480. Render ratio 1:1 world movement, with source origin at cell top-left and no repetition. The visible body and wheels should fit **within** the cell, leaving transparent breathing room at sides/top; at least one grounded wheel contact should reach the last native row or be justified in a registered review. It must sit behind existing foreground ground and actors. Image tool may generate another size: preserve original, exact prompt, dimensions and hash, then deliberately crop/resample to the candidate cell, disclose transform and inspect at native 1×.
