## Proportional mapping and alpha

The unmodified RGBA master is 2172 × 724, SHA-256 `7308dc13dcf588a7ee0cfbb085ac962d56d311c294d306dbfe8ac664840fb873`. Width-fit Lanczos resize yields 1920 × 640, then a top-left crop yields 1920 × 576; full width and upper-left origin are retained, and the lower 64 resized pixels are excluded. The 3:1 source and 10:3 target cannot both be filled without crop, padding or distortion; this preserves aspect, not exact registration of all authored features to the guide. No masks, painting, alpha cleanup or source edit was applied.

Source alpha row 641 contains substantial wall pixels; row 642 peaks at alpha 126, row 643 is zero, and rows 644–647 contain only faint alpha ≤7. In source rows 652–723, 43 pixels are nonzero, all alpha 1; all others are zero. The crop therefore discards a transparent band and negligible alpha noise, rather than visibly substantial wall. The exact fractional resampling boundary and interpolation footprint are recorded by the transform; do not treat this as perfect geometric registration.

- `camera_x0_y18_native_960.png` and its y0 companion show the real sump/warning in front of a continuous dark rear wall. The exposed foliage cutout seen in v01 is no longer apparent. At the very bottom, normal foreground and HUD obscure part of the view; a still is not proof of hazard readability during play.

Large building piers and diagonal braces give architectural context, but exact structural alignment to each of the four authored spans is only approximate. Several long light-edged rooflines and pipes run horizontally near gameplay surfaces; they compete visually with the walkable edges and could imply false ledges. Test while moving and jumping; this is a readability concern, not evidence of incorrect collision.
- `camera_x240_y18_native_960.png` shows the existing drone's red targeting line and warning over the facade. The mast is visible over the tall building, but its lower edge appears suspended in front of the wall rather than visibly connected to a mount; check support and attachment through the other camera position. Lamps and warning are currently distinguishable in this single captured state.
- `camera_x480_y18_native_960.png` shows the real right airlock and foreground cover without the earlier rectangular foliage hole. Mateo's dark olive silhouette has limited contrast against adjacent masonry at this location. The bright upper far-layer rail and rear roofline remain possible false-ledge cues. Exit interaction and player movement are untested.
- The six frames represent positions, not a continuous camera pan, animation, projectile range, CRT-on review or all enemy/cover configurations. Check actual collision, exit use, mast support, sump/hazard, HUD and telegraphs through real input; inspect both CRT states and real display before any promotion.
