# Controls and combat accessibility — working contract

**Status: implementation in progress, not an acceptance report.** This is a user-requested bounded increment alongside the roadmap's existing gates. It does not mark R01/R02 or any milestone complete. See [current state](../../../CURRENT_STATE.md) and [roadmap](../../../TODO_ROADMAP.md).

## Player-facing behavior

- A fresh settings file uses **Assisted** combat. Settings from schema 1 or 2 migrate in memory to **Classic**, retaining existing volume, CRT and scale without rewriting on read. A prominent pause-settings control switches profile and persists a valid change; failed writes leave the last good settings and behavior intact. New/Continue must not erase the chosen setting.
- Mouse movement aims in game/world space; for the starter maser, left mouse or controller right trigger repeats normal shots while held in either profile. Other unlocked weapons retain their own authored press/hold/release behavior. Existing X/J/Left Ctrl and right mouse or controller left trigger retain deliberate press/hold/release charge semantics. Forced input cancellation on pause, focus loss, disconnect, handoff, profile change or modal closure must not discharge a held charge.
- Assisted starter maser fires one center shot of damage 50 and two flank shots of damage 20 at ±10 degrees, on 0.16-second cadence. Resolve a volley as at most one hit per target, center first. Classic preserves its original single 25-damage shot and 0.20-second cadence. Existing enemy/boss vulnerability, cover, weapon unlocks, player health, movement/collider and world geometry remain authoritative.
- Standard controller gameplay mapping: A jump, B dash, X interact, Y grapple, left bumper weapon cycle, right bumper ability, Start pause, Back dossier. Left stick moves; right stick aims with a dead zone. Basic title, bunker and pause navigation are in scope; advanced panels and free-text communication may require keyboard handoff. Controller-only campaign completion, physical device compatibility, remapping and full R02 acceptance are not claimed without their separate checks.

## Current visual and device boundary

The integrated field and boss routes use an aim reticle and actual projectile vector; the existing Mateo sprite changes facing but does not acquire a new angled weapon pose. The separate enlarged recoil concept is not installed. Preliminary captures and synthetic controller events are fixtures; do not imply real-window play, physical-controller compatibility or continuous human motion. The current implementation remains under review until the final checkpoint; this section is a behavior target and scope boundary, not acceptance evidence.

## Integration and ownership

Aim coordinates must account for actual window scale, viewport, camera and both facings. Synchronize facing/weapon origin with pointer or stick aim before spawning; test the real previous-animation-facing case. The field and boss must use consistent actions and profiles. Preserve pause, relay panel, 1977 flashback and text-entry focus; no button or mouse event may leak a shot into gameplay. Device switches and disconnect must clear stale held inputs and cancel charge without firing.

Researcher owns `engine/input.py` and separate pointer/controller mapper tests. Combat owner owns `engine/weapons.py`, `data/balance.json` and separate combat-profile tests. Technical owner owns `player.py`, scenes, state machine, settings and integration-specific runtime tests. Director owns separate `tests/test_accessible_controls_integration.py` plus `CONTROLS.md` and shared planning documentation. No owner edits another's files without coordination. Existing untracked files require hash-verified recovery copies before editing; new tests use temporary saves and explicit fresh output directories.

Acceptance uses real `pygame` events through the game state machine in field and boss, both profiles and actual mouse/controller action pathways; dummy SDL and fake controller enumeration are fixtures, not physical-hardware claims. Include pointer conversion at both integer scales with camera movement, aim quadrants and coincidence, controller dead-zone/neutral and disconnect, repeated fire/charge, target and boss vulnerability guards, profile migration/persistence/write failure, focus/pause/modal cancellation, New/Continue isolation, and original Classic regression. Verify visible instructions and game captures; any earned route, human play, native window, physical controller or release-gate claim needs separate evidence.
