# Controls and combat accessibility — bounded implementation report

The requested controls and starter-maser assistance are implemented. The [player controls](../../../../CONTROLS.md) explain the profile choice and input mappings. Fresh settings default to Assisted; existing schema 1/2 settings load as Classic without a read-time rewrite. F2 → COMBAT switches and persists the profile. Mouse aim and starter-maser left-button repeat/right-button charge, mapped-controller aim/trigger controls, and the Assisted center/flank volley are integrated in field and boss. Other weapons retain their own firing behavior. Pause, focus, device and modal cancellation protect against stale held input; no new character animation or world/collider change is implied.
