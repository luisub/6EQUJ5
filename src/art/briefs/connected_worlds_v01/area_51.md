# Area 51 perimeter: first candidate

One original 1920×540 opaque nighttime desert plate, side elevation, native world-floor target y=480. Design uses a wide playa and distant Nevada ranges, fictional modest hangars, generic fence, utility equipment, 1990s utility vehicles and a handful of distant staff. Isolated amber and cyan lamps articulate the cool blue-steel darkness. Sky and lower strip remain quiet; no hero or UI is baked in. The CIA historical description and inspected historic U-2 photograph in `references/world_expansion_v01/{README,SOURCES}.md` support only the historic dry lake, test context, vehicles and mountain setting; this game's buildings, security arrangement and 2029 incident are imagined. No reference photograph was used as source pixels.

The native study has strong local material and lighting detail, but upper sky consumes a large fraction of the crop. The fence, tank, bollards, vehicles and hangar entrances could read as gameplay geometry. The foreground floor line is approximately y=480–490, and actual integration must establish contact and distinguish the background from collidable objects. Nearest noninteger reduction may introduce uneven pixel clusters. No in-game, pan, CRT, or human-play evaluation has been performed. Await root decision before promotion or further areas.
