# Synchronicity: single boss-body key pose

An original 256×192 RGBA pose at 128×96 world units for the fictional Neo Denver array warden: left-facing compact treads, three unequal radio dishes on distinct copper actuators, ceramic insulators, worn navy armor and a cyan-white emitter. This is a static concept only: motion, attacks, vulnerable states, telegraphs, collision and gameplay integration remain open.

V01 clipped the subject at the source edges; v02 gives the silhouette margins but leaves the emitter low; v03 raises it. Final nonzero-alpha bounds `(18,10,237,188)` leave a nominal y=188 baseline. The emitter is approximately `(143,105)`, right of the brief's `(110,95)`. Clear bottom rows are four, since a y=188 baseline cannot coexist with six empty bottom rows in a 192-pixel-high cell. The three dishes read at native size, although the smallest is small; bright antenna faces may compete with the HUD and must be tested in an actual boss arena. One static fixture composites this candidate over the Neo Denver plate; it is not actual gameplay or motion.
