# Vulture Echo Guardian — one-pose concept v01

**Concept only.** A fictional autonomous stone-and-bronze guardian for a possible imagined Göbekli pillar approach beyond conserved archaeology. The animal motif is informed by DAI's identification of a vulture on Pillar 43, as documented in `references/world_expansion_v01/villains.md` and `gobekli_animal_sources.md`. This is not a reconstruction, a copied relief, an interpretation of ancient meaning, a playable enemy or a replacement for Core Prime and Archon of Silence. Act V and its blockout remain future work.

## Scale and composition

Actual current field runtime measurement, dummy SDL, new game direct field entry after 30 updates: idle frame 0 at density 2 and scale 0.25 gives a **48×64 native cell**, visible alpha >127 bounds `(11,4,25,59)` (x, y, width, height), the same bounds for alpha >0, and logical foot pivot `(12,31)` = native `(24,62)`. Thus visible Mateo height is **59 native pixels**, not cell height 64. One representative planted vulture pose targets visible height about **74 native pixels** (1.25×59), folded width about **89 pixels** (1.2×74); a prospective later opened wing span is about **100 pixels** (1.7×59). Actual size and fit are reviewed from the resulting bitmap, not asserted for the generator. Concept export study: 192×128 native RGBA cell, prospective 96×64 logical world units only; no collider, physics, animation or runtime implication. Place feet on one stable visual baseline, with open negative space beneath the chest.

## Art direction

One original side-view planted pose, small hooked forward head and a single restrained amber/cyan lens, compact bird body, two short planted legs, broad layered wedge wings just beginning to lift. Warm chalk limestone-like plates, chipped edges, deep umber seams and aged bronze hinges; clear silhouette and deliberate pixel clusters, no noise, photorealism, mural, pillar, monument, copied relief, orb or inscription. A solid construction with readable plate joints, not a skeletal bird or human figure. A hypothetical wing-lift telegraph and low glide need future animated testing; this single pose proves neither. Transparent background, isolated subject, no baseline, shadow plane, text, HUD, weapons, extra subjects or pose sheet.
