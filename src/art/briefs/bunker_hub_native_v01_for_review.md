# Bunker hub — native background proposal v01

**Status: proposed for Astra review; one artist request only after review.** User authorized improving existing scenes; this first bunker component is a **new** candidate family, not a replacement of the six locked bunker layers or the original masters. Art direction is the established retrofuturist observatory control room: tape racks, scientific receiver, lab bench, repair electronics and sealed surface airlock. It must feel like a lived-in working interior, not the exterior service yard, scrap depot or reflector arena.

## Actual view and interaction contract

`BunkerHubScene` is a fixed **480×270 logical** view, rendered to **960×540** at density 2; there is no scrolling camera. It loads six existing 480×270 layers from `assets/bunker/scene_manifest.json` via `AssetCache.bunker_layers()`. Layers 01 distant, 02 rear wall, 03 equipment and 04 playable floor render before the existing player shadow and 96×128 **logical** bunker strip cell at `(mateo_x,122)`; layers 05 foreground and 06 lighting render above Mateo; prompts, save notice, HUD and dossier render afterward. The bunker player representation is separate from the field 48×64 native cell: do not use the field-scale preview or change its size, animation or foot.

Actual native registration fixtures are in `previews/prototype_geometry_research_v01/capture_v01/`: flattened six-layer guide, colored station trigger bands and direct-positioned screenshots for each station, using temporary saves. The flattened guide is reference only because layers 05–06 actually render after the actor. The repair and field prompt strings visibly clip at the right edge in current fixtures; this is a separately reported UI issue, not permission to redesign the interaction.

Station trigger ranges test **the sprite's `mateo_x` coordinate**, not the visible sprite center. Preserve them and their current semantic associations: lab `124–160`, receiver `170–260`, repair `275–315`, surface airlock `330–364` logical. Mateo can traverse between these anchors. His cell is 96×128 logical at y=122, so actor screen envelope changes with position and may cross foreground equipment. Do not turn an artwork position into a relocated interaction, block movement, hide a workstation's purpose or imply an extra usable station.

Top HUD and bottom footer are screen-fixed; save text sits near logical y=228, and station prompts are dynamically placed near y=100 logical with horizontal position following Mateo. Dossier, progression/repair messages, CRT-on/off and pause may cover more. Keep backdrop detail and light behind text quiet; do not paint any letters, generated pseudotext, HUD, interaction labels, characters, shadow, dialogue, enemies or cursor into the art.

## One first component

Create **one coherent opaque 960×540 native backdrop** for the present fixed room, intended as a candidate replacement for the combined visual content of layers 01–04 only. It includes the background hull, rear wall, equipment and actual playable floor but **not** separate foreground ducts, post-player lighting, player or UI. Existing scene order and station triggers remain authoritative. This is an authored study for a new room-specific family; technical must define how to replace only those four layers while keeping player, prompts and HUD at their current depths. Existing layers 05–06 should initially be retained for a registered comparison, but they may misalign or double-light the new plate; disclose that result and revise or separately commission later rather than calling a mismatch acceptable. Do not flatten a screenshot or silently replace a locked asset.

- **Left:** recognizable tall analog tape racks and scientific instrumentation, dark structural framing and meaningful material shapes. Avoid dense tiny indicator noise or new readable lettering.
- **Central:** distinctive green phosphor receiver and validation/science work area with clear actual interaction identity. Controls, screens, notes, cables and metal surfaces should form readable clusters, not a wall of unrelated rectangles. Keep enough contrast behind Mateo across his walk range and leave native prompt positions quiet.
- **Right:** warm repair electronics, grounded workbench and recognizable sealed airlock in the established positions; practical directional amber fixtures describe form against cool deep wall shadows, without glowing areas masquerading as actionable UI.
- **Floor / ceiling:** retain the existing side-on walking plane and foot contact around the runtime y=122+124 pivot; authored cable/concrete joints can enrich it but cannot invent obstacles. Restrained dark overhead structure gives foreground depth without obscuring actor, critical panels or text. Any true post-player foreground is deferred as a separate layer, not baked into this first background.

The desired light/dark contrast should be as deliberate as the approved exterior direction—strong shadow, shaped green phosphor and selective amber practicals—but its material hierarchy and warm/cool pools must serve workstations and text rather than combat. Keep 1977-inspired equipment plausible, preserve the site's existing identity and view, and avoid a copy of the concept image or placeholder text.

## Native asset and review

Proposed output `bunker_background_v01_960x540.png`: exactly **960×540**, fully opaque, fixed origin `(0,0)`, density 2, nonrepeating. User-facing composition remains the same room and framing. The image tool may yield a larger master: preserve original and exact prompt/output dimensions/hash, then derive the native candidate with a documented proportional crop/resample and 1× cleanup/review. Do not claim exact native generation if unobserved; do not substitute procedurally manufactured bitmap art or an upscaled old plate for authored detail. Preserve `src/art/source/bunker/` masters, all `assets/bunker/` exports and the locked builder group.

Before promotion, obtain actual native fixture frames with Mateo at each station, prompt, save notice and dossier; check station interaction, actor feet/shadow and upper/lower overlay against both old and candidate background. Compare CRT off/on, scrolling dossier and full receiver/lab transitions; review light/dark, 960 native, nearest 1920 and movement playback. In temporary-save tests use normal input where feasible and disclose direct placements. A static plate cannot demonstrate interaction, actor occlusion, prompt fit, human readability, native performance or G2/G3 acceptance. A new candidate family requires explicit source/hash, complete fallback and safe transaction design; it must not silently overwrite `scene_manifest.json` or the existing bunker build outputs.
