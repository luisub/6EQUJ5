# Fitzsimons-inspired campus: visual research

This is reference research for one **fictional Ohio campus** scene, not a claim that Fitzsimons is in Ohio or that the game depicts its actual campus. Reference images are not production assets. Do not copy signs, logos, photographs or exact campus geography.

- [User's Denverite Express page](https://denveriteexpress.wordpress.com/2017/12/06/fitzsimons-army-hospital/) includes historical and contemporary views of Building 500. Its [contemporary exterior image](https://denveriteexpress.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/dsc_0574.jpg?w=636) shows the broad, horizontally banded masonry facade and stepped center. The directing agent described the user-supplied photograph as a long symmetrical tan-brick facade, three tall vertical center window strips, pale recessed entrance, metal window grids, trees and flagpole; that attachment was not separately available to this researcher. These are visual cues, not a demand for a literal reconstruction.
- [Denver Public Library Special Collections and Archives](https://history.denverlibrary.org/building/fitzsimons-general-hospital) describes the main building as Modernistic and confirms a stepped, terraced plan. Its exterior photograph offers an independent view of the massing. [CU Anschutz's 2025 energy master plan](https://www.cuanschutz.edu/docs/librariesprovider260/sustainability/cu-anschutz-energy-master-plan_2025.pdf) describes exterior brick and stone walls and original windows. These sources support massing and materials; lighting, campus arrangement, palette, and the fictional Ohio location are art direction.

Observed scene comparison: the existing campus native fixture `previews/ohio_campus_extension_v01/west_revision_review_v02/campus_x240_y18_native_960.png` has a small dome and low red-brick service buildings under repeated large overhead trestles, with lamps, fence and a van. The later actual-root dummy-driver fixture `planning/evidence/PLAY_FLOW/2026-11-12_longer_easier/integration/earned_route_v06/act_01_ohio_campus_native_crt_off.png` shows the low skyline behind combat. These are static fixtures, not evidence of a human playthrough. A taller stepped landmark and more varied campus silhouette address the requested direction; the trestle belongs to the existing far plate and remains a compositing constraint.

Research conducted 2026-11-12. Image tool work, rights clearance, and gameplay verification are separate work; none is claimed here.
