# Greenkeeper reflector basin — full-room brief v01

**Status: proposed; no generation before Astra review.** This is the actual existing Act I boss arena, not a new campaign area. Its identity is a vast, industrial **deep reflector basin**: heavy structural walls and grounded steel, severe near-black shadow masses, selective warm service lights, restrained cool ambient and an imposing but quiet mechanical backdrop. It must be visually distinct from approach's warm masonry, salvage's outdoor storage and the dish catwalk's exposed feedhorn. The boss, weak point, telegraphs and movement are the primary composition, not decorative machinery.

Do not hide or reorder those elements for a background pass. This boss caller is distinct from the field-scene loader; a future per-room native-art family must explicitly support this path and fallback without globally replacing shared Ohio assets.

## Preserve actual geometry and combat zones

World coordinates; double them for a native room-aligned source. Ground is continuous y=240 with side walls x=0–32 and x=608–640 and ceiling y=0–16. One-way tops: x=128–288 and x=352–512 at y=144; x=64–192 and x=416–544 at y=192. True code-4 cover tiles occupy x=224–256 and x=384–416 at y=224–240; corresponding cover objects are defined there. Left gate terminal `(32,192,16,48)`, pre-boss checkpoint `(64,208)` and right transit exit `(576,192,32,48)` are actual locations. Preserve exact visual/collision semantics; no new platform, ledge, false exit, low cover or hazard. Actor/boss movement, bullets, mortar impact markers, sweeping attacks, weak point and HUD are dynamic across the frame; no world-coordinate still image can certify them safe.

Greenkeeper has mortar windups, high arcs and overhead vulnerability; sweep/charge phases use low horizontal attack lanes, and later states have different exposures and timing. Keep upper and mid screen comparatively quiet for projectiles and target warnings, retain strong body/weak-point silhouette contrast behind the actual boss at x≈460, and make the real low cover and true platform lips immediately distinguishable. Boss health/phase bar and conditional lock-on/prompt panels are screen fixed; no bright architectural pattern behind their text. Do not bake warning markers, glow, attack states or other false gameplay cues into the art.

## One-layer-at-a-time candidate strategy

A future first authored component may be one **1280×576 straight-alpha RGBA world-fixed** scene layer, origin world `(0,0)`, density 2, crop `(2cx,2cy,960,540)` at actual camera x=0–160/y=0–18, finite and nonrepeating. Far sky may be a separately reviewed fixture or a later room-specific plane; do not assume approach's selected far is globally correct. Art belongs behind existing room geometry and both actors, with no fake ground, raised playable edges or clipped alpha cutouts. Exposed monumental reflector curvature, restrained braces behind the four real spans and weathered steel/concrete create an arena-scale silhouette. Use one or two motivated, localized warm service lights in the margins, never a giant orange or red burst competing with boss warnings. Natural transparent gaps above structure are okay; do not carve holes around game objects.

Any image generator may return a different source size: preserve its original, actual dimensions, prompt and hash; review source geometry before a disclosed proportional conversion to 1280×576. One artist, one reviewed layer at a time. No procedural bitmap stand-in or global Ohio tileset replacement. This brief does not authorize new art until reviewed.
