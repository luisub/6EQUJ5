# Big Ear 1977 v01 — registered review and targeted correction

**Recommendation: revise before integration.** The period workroom, printer location, observation window, cabinet and restrained warm light are useful. However, the independent native fixture shows the furniture and cabinet apparently suspended above the real ground. This is a concrete registration defect rather than a judgment on the whole room or its gameplay.

`previews/ehman_1977_registration_director_v01/capture_v01/desk_before_y18_native_960.png` and `vault_before_retrieval_y18_native_960.png` show furniture bottoms near screen y=421 while the actual rendered floor begins at y=444: approximately 23 native pixels of blank wall beneath them. Real printer and vault zones do align horizontally with their respective source objects, so preserve that context rather than relocating the whole composition.

The printer's pale blank paper reads as inert printer stock in the source, not a baked signal or annotation; the actual paper and annotation are separately rendered. In the composite, green terminal glyphs and `AIRLOCK` are existing runtime visuals, not features painted by this candidate. The generated source does contain copied side tile strips; remove these in a correction because actual room side tiles are already drawn separately. Do not infer a finished interaction from appearance alone.

## Proposed single correction

Retain the source's exact full-room framing, overall composition, warm/cool lighting, printer, desk, bookshelves, window and vault cabinet. Extend their individual legs or bases naturally down to **native source y=480** behind the actual ground; do not add a continuous floor, plinth, raised platform or lip at the previous y≈457. Keep printer controls identifiable within the existing native context x=192–256/y=416–480 and cabinet context x=736–800/y=416–480, while keeping actual player and interactive markers in front. Remove copied side tile strips and continue ordinary background behind the real room boundaries. The blank printer stock may remain inert; do not add visible signal text, annotation, an extracted reel, status lights or other stage-dependent information. Preserve original source, output and hashes in a new candidate family; this proposal does not itself request or generate an edit.

After correction, repeat desk/vault registered views at both camera crops and actual inspect/annotate/reel states; verify complete normal-input route, return, save, actor depth, camera and fallback separately. Static fixtures do not establish full playthrough, CRT/native-window quality, performance, human acceptance or art-gate closure.
