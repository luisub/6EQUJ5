## Purpose and visual identity

An **outdoor decommissioned Big Ear surplus yard**: deep, cool shadow masses and selectively amber-lit stored radio machinery, battered sheds, fenced scrap and depot equipment. The user approved rich strong light/dark contrast; make this a recognizable, layered place with several coherent depth clusters and meaningful material definition rather than the approach's long brick garage wall or a sparse flat silhouette. Use oxidized steel, canvas, weathered concrete, copper cable and worn enamel as distinct materials, with purposeful practical lamps and cast shadow shapes. Place large masses behind a clear action corridor; do not flood the room with mist, speckle, glowing outlines or invented UI.
