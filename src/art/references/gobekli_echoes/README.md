# Scene villains and animal directions v02

Research and design notes, 2026-11-12. This deepens the [v01 roster](../world_expansion/villains.md); it is not a new runtime roster, a sprite commission, or an approval of enemy mechanics. Ohio has playable rooms; the concepts below are proposals even there. Later acts remain planned, including all Act V enemies. Keep the canonical bosses, story and faction identities. The architectural references and setting limits are in the [v01 world packet](../world_expansion/README.md); the archaeological facts and actual image-viewing record are in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

Proposed material, motion and warning cues below are design inventions. Preserve the player's readable lane and existing objectives; any range, timing, hitbox, counter and encounter composition needs normal-input, animation and fairness testing. Do not place hostile characters as real archaeologists, workers or representatives of institutions. Heritage references inform material vocabulary, not a claim that ancient people made machines or believed in these guardians.

## Additional animal options, not placements

- **Boar:** a broad, low, blunt-shouldered original construct could provide a grounded charge silhouette. Use DAI's distinct low-relief boar on C Pillar 27 as a motif anchor; the nearby high-relief predator is a separate animal and a hunting interpretation remains a question. Bright shoulder lift and a marked short lane would precede any charge; recovery must leave room for escape.
- **Bull:** an optional heavy silhouette with paired horn-like antennae and a broad front plate could suit an imaginary late-game machine. DAI uses “bull” for A Pillar 2; UNESCO's nomination is more specific. Horns, armor and attack are design choices, not a reconstruction. Lower the head and mark a lane before movement, then pause.
- These are alternatives for future review, not additional bosses, archaeological facts about combat, or implied approved art. Don't copy the source reliefs' poses, arrangements or weathering.
