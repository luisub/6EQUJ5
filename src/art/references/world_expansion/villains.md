# Scene-specific villains — original concepts v01

Concepts only, 2026-11-12. This extends the [world research](README.md) with one location-specific visual/behavioral role per planned room. The user has authorized creating scene-based villains; the current art review is one isolated Vulture Echo Guardian concept queued after the Ohio environment. These are not existing sprites, implemented enemies, approved character designs, difficulty measurements, or approval for a batch of art. All later-act environments and encounters require their planned blockout and acceptance; P01a remains planned after S11 and Act V is not playable. Do not replace the existing story, approved cast, reviewed Greenkeeper, canonical bosses or faction identities. Proposed forms are scene-specific variants or fictional adjuncts of Core machinery, Static Hounds, Phase-Watchers, Chrysalis Splicers, Simian Precursors and Temporal Phantoms; they do not claim those groups already have these capabilities in the runtime. Real institutions and heritage workers are not designated villains. Exact sizes, attacks, counters and timing need animation/readability/fairness testing before adoption.

## One visual benchmark for a future Act V review

**Vulture Echo Guardian — original fantasy interpretation of one animal motif.** DAI identifies the large bird on Enclosure D Pillar 43 as a vulture. The inspected [DAI relief photo](https://www.dainst.org/_assets/4/a/csm_pillar-43-scaled_73bcaa226a.jpg), [research article](https://www.dainst.org/en/blogs/of-animals-and-a-headless-man-goebekli-tepe-pillar-43/599), and [UNESCO photo record](https://whc.unesco.org/en/documents/165846) are visual references; see [animal source ledger](gobekli_animal_sources.md). No meaning is assigned to the ancient composition. This character is a new machine/construct inhabiting an **imagined** extension of the site; bronze and its animation are fictional design elements, not archaeological claims.

- **Silhouette and scale:** broad wedge-shaped folded wings, compact forward-set hooked bird head, two short legs and a distinct negative space beneath the chest. Standing height target about **1.25× Mateo’s visible height**; opened wings roughly **1.7× Mateo’s height wide**. These are relative concept targets, not collision-box or export dimensions.
- **Palette and material:** warm chalk limestone-like plates with chipped edges, deep umber seams and restrained aged-bronze joints; a small cyan-amber warning lens separates the telegraph from the stone backdrop. The construct is distinct from actual pillars, with no copied relief panel, orb, inscription or recognizable archaeological damage.
- **Movement and combat:** slow planted pivot; wing plates visibly rise and a bounded floor strip appears; short committed low glide, landing pause, then vulnerable joint. Player steps out of the strip or interrupts in windup; the post-landing opening must be long enough to read in testing. Avoid swoops from offscreen, indistinct flashes and collision extending beyond the visible body.
- **Artist prompt:** “Create one original side-view 2D pixel-art enemy concept for a retrofuturist action game: a compact vulture-inspired autonomous guardian made from layered warm limestone-like armour and aged bronze hinges, broad folded wedge wings, small hooked head, short legs, visible gap under chest, a single restrained amber/cyan warning lens. Show one representative standing pose with the wings just beginning to rise into a warning, against a plain neutral background. Draw an original construct, not a photograph, pillar, carving, sacred symbol, known character or collage; no copied archaeological composition, no text, no gore. Approximate standing height 1.25 times the existing player’s visible height; final pixel dimensions and collider are undecided.”
