Research date: 2026-11-12. Scholarly identification is reported at the specificity the sources use. These images document ancient human-made work, not proof of magical guardians, astronomical codes or a fixed religious interpretation. UNESCO describes monumental limestone buildings from the Pre-Pottery Neolithic, with wild-animal reliefs, and dates the property broadly to approximately 9600–8200 BCE: [UNESCO property description](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1572/). That range does not independently date each depicted carving. The [Turkish Ministry of Culture and Tourism site page](https://muze.gov.tr/muze-detay?amp=&distId=SGT&sectionId=SGT01) also lists animal motifs including fox, wild boar, scorpion and snake; it does not supply a precise pillar assignment for each in that summary. Neither the site’s meaning nor the motifs’ functions should be presented as settled. Specific planned encounters below are future fantasy interpretations, not claims about the site's past or present inhabitants.
